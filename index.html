Our Philosophy

Great learning is
deeply personal

“Every child learns differently. Every child deserves a tutor who truly understands them.”

At TutorKlub, we built our entire model around one conviction: the biggest problem with online tutoring is not technology — it is anonymity. Too many platforms pack twenty or thirty students into a session, rush through a fixed curriculum, and move on. Every child becomes a face in a grid.

We do the opposite. Our classes never exceed five students. Every tutor is hand-picked, rigorously trained, and matched thoughtfully to each group. We layer in AI tools not to replace human connection, but to make our tutors far more effective — giving them real-time insight into exactly where each child is struggling.

Our Curriculum

Courses & Programmes

Structured for Grades 1–5 and designed for adult learners. Each course is a living curriculum — shaped by your child’s needs, not a fixed template.

🔢
Mathematics
Grades 1 – 5
Ages 6–11

We build genuine mathematical thinking — not just procedure drilling. From number sense in Grade 1 through to fractions, decimals, and data interpretation in Grade 5, every concept is taught with clarity and connected to real-world understanding.

  • Grade 1–2: Number sense, counting, addition & subtraction, shapes & patterns
  • Grade 3: Multiplication, division, measurement, introductory fractions
  • Grade 4: Multi-digit operations, equivalent fractions, area & perimeter
  • Grade 5: Decimals, ratios, data handling, geometry & problem-solving
  • AI-generated practice sets tailored to each student between sessions
50 min · twice weekly
📚
English
Grades 1 – 5
Ages 6–11

Our English programme develops confident readers and writers from the ground up. We focus on phonics and decoding in the early grades, building progressively into comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, and structured writing.

  • Grade 1–2: Phonics, sight words, fluency reading, basic sentence construction
  • Grade 3: Reading comprehension, parts of speech, paragraph writing
  • Grade 4: Narrative & descriptive writing, vocabulary expansion, punctuation
  • Grade 5: Essay structure, persuasive writing, literary analysis
  • Monthly reading challenges with tutor-led group discussions
50 min · twice weekly
ㄢ
Telugu
Grades 1 – 5
All levels

Whether your child is a complete beginner or grew up hearing Telugu at home, our programme builds genuine fluency and literacy. We cover script recognition, reading, writing, spoken communication, and cultural context.

  • Varnamala: letter recognition, vowels & consonants, aksharas
  • Reading: simple words → sentences → short paragraphs
  • Writing: matras, compound letters, dictation exercises
  • Spoken Telugu: conversational phrases, storytelling, role-play
  • Cultural connections: festivals, songs, poems & folk tales
50 min · twice weekly
ह
Hindi
Grades 1 – 5
All levels

Hindi opens doors to culture, family, and connection across India. Our Devanagari-based curriculum takes children from recognising their first letters to reading short stories and holding simple conversations, all in a warm, encouraging small-group setting.

  • Devanagari script: swar (vowels), vyanjan (consonants), matra practice
  • Reading: two-letter words → sentences → short paragraphs
  • Writing: dictation, copy-writing, creative sentence building
  • Spoken Hindi: greetings, numbers, colours, family & daily life
  • Hindi rhymes, poems, and short stories for Grades 1–2
50 min · twice weekly
ㄢ
Telugu for Adults
Beginners to Intermediate
Adults

For heritage speakers wanting to regain fluency, and for complete beginners. Our adult Telugu programme focuses on practical spoken communication, script literacy, and cultural depth — in flexible evening and weekend slots around busy schedules.

  • Conversational Telugu: everyday situations, formal & informal registers
  • Script literacy: reading & writing at your own pace
  • Grammar essentials: verb conjugation, tense, sentence building
  • Vocabulary themes: family, travel, food, workplace, current events
  • Cultural immersion: literature, cinema, idioms & proverbs
60 min · once or twice weekly

Beginners to Intermediate
Adults

Built for learners who want to speak confidently — whether you are reconnecting with a family language, preparing for travel to India, or simply taking on a new challenge. Lessons are conversational-first, with grammar woven in naturally.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Five students is the sweet spot between the social energy of a group and the personal attention of a one-to-one session. In a class of five, every child speaks in every session, every child is heard, and the tutor can genuinely track what each student understands in real time. It is a deliberate, non-negotiable decision — not a limitation we are working around.

Every TutorKlub tutor goes through a multi-stage selection process that includes a subject knowledge assessment, a sample teaching demonstration, and a background check. Before their first class, tutors complete a structured in-house training programme covering child-centred pedagogy, the TutorKlub curriculum framework, and how to use our AI coaching tools effectively. Training is ongoing — tutors participate in monthly review sessions with our academic team.

Our AI runs entirely in the background during sessions. It analyses response patterns and engagement signals, then surfaces insights to the tutor on a private dashboard — students never see it or interact with it. After class, it generates personalised practice materials and the parent progress report automatically. The AI never speaks to your child directly; it simply makes the human tutor far more informed and effective.

All classes are held on Google Meet. Your child needs any device with a working camera and microphone — a laptop, tablet, or desktop computer works best. A mobile phone can work but a larger screen makes it easier to see the shared whiteboard. When you book a trial class through our Calendly link, a unique Google Meet link is automatically sent to your email within seconds. You just click the link at the scheduled time — no Google account required for students to join.

Immediately. The moment you complete your booking via our Calendly scheduling page, Calendly automatically generates a unique Google Meet link and emails it directly to you. You will also receive a calendar invite with the Google Meet link embedded. A reminder is sent 24 hours before the class and again one hour before. If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder for a message from notifications@calendly.com.

For the free trial class, we encourage parents to sit in for the first few minutes to see the format and settle any nerves your child might have. After that, we gently suggest parents let children participate independently — children tend to engage more freely and ask more questions when they feel the session is their own space. You will receive a detailed post-class report regardless, so you are never in the dark about what happened.

Absolutely. Our language courses are designed for all levels, including complete beginners who have never seen the script before. During the trial class, our tutor assesses your child’s starting point and matches them with a group at a similar level. There is no pressure to “catch up” with anyone — every class starts exactly where each student is.

All classes are conducted in English, with the target language introduced progressively. For Math and English classes, instruction is entirely in English. For Telugu and Hindi classes, the tutor uses English to explain concepts and instructions while building the child’s target language exposure gradually. For adult language learners, the balance shifts toward the target language as proficiency grows.

No contracts, no lock-ins. We work on a month-to-month basis, and you can cancel or pause at any time with seven days notice before your next billing cycle. We believe families should stay because they are seeing results — not because they are contractually obligated to. That said, we do recommend committing to at least six to eight weeks to give the curriculum time to produce measurable results.

We understand that life happens. If you give us 24 hours notice, we will do our best to schedule a make-up session or credit your account for a future class. Every child also receives post-session notes and AI-generated practice materials after every class, so missing one session never means falling permanently behind. Repeated absences without notice cannot be credited, but we will always try to find a solution that works for your family.

Our adult programme currently focuses on Telugu and Hindi language learning. Math and English courses are designed specifically for Grades 1–5. If you are an adult interested in language skills, please choose our dedicated adult language tracks — they are built for your pace, your schedule, and your goals as a grown-up learner.

If you are not satisfied after your first paid month, contact us within 7 days of your billing date and we will issue a full refund, no questions asked. We stand behind the quality of our teaching. After the first month, refunds are calculated on a pro-rata basis for unused classes if you cancel mid-month with valid notice.
