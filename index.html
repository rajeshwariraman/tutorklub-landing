Our Philosophy

Your child doesn’t learn
like everyone else.
We teach the way they do.

“Every child learns differently. Every child deserves to be seen in every class.”

At TutorKlub, we built our entire model around one conviction: great learning is deeply personal. We don’t teach to a syllabus. We teach to the child in front of us.

✨
Confidence before content. A child who believes they can will always outperform a child who doesn’t. We build that belief first.
🤝
We never belittle. Every question is a good question. Every mistake is a learning moment. No child is made to feel small in our class.
🧠
Emotional resilience. We build children who can sit with difficulty, try again, and grow. That skill follows them for life.
🔎
Inference-based & adaptive reasoning. We don’t just ask “what is the answer?” We ask “how did you get there?” Thinking is the skill, not recall.
🌟
The result is teaching that feels personal. Because it is.
The Journey

Every child learns differently.
We teach the way yours thinks.

From your first demo to a personalised learning path — here is exactly what to expect.

1

Book a Free Demo Class

Choose a subject and time that works for your family. Your Google Meet link is sent automatically the moment you book — no waiting, no back-and-forth.

🚌 Google Meet · 30 minutes · Free
2

Attend the Demo — 1-on-1 with Your Tutor

Your child gets a dedicated 30-minute 1-on-1 session with the tutor. No other students — just your child and the tutor, so we can see exactly how they engage, think, and learn.

3

Enrol & Diagnostic Assessment

If you love it, enrol your child. On enrolment and monthly fee payment, your child completes a short structured assessment matched to their grade and subject. We test how they think — not just what they know at their grade level. We look at reasoning patterns, where thinking breaks down, and what that tells us about how they learn.

📋 Full written report + personal tutor call
4

Foundation Plan (if needed)

If the assessment reveals gaps, we recommend a personalised Foundation Programme — 8 private sessions to fix the essentials and build a strong base. You choose whether to take it up. No pressure either way.

⚡ 8 sessions · Private 1-on-1 · Gap-focused
5

Placed in the Right Batch

Based on assessment results, your child is placed in the Regular Batch or Advanced Batch — with students at the same level, moving at the right pace. No child is put into a group before they are ready.

🌟 Regular Batch or Advanced Batch

Our Curriculum

Courses & Programmes

Structured for Grades 1–5, Ages 12–15, and Adult Learners. Each course is built around your child’s needs — not a fixed template.

🔢
Mathematics
Grades 1–5 · Ages 6–11
Grades 1–5

We build genuine mathematical thinking — not just procedure drilling. From number sense in Grade 1 through fractions, decimals, and data interpretation in Grade 5, every concept is taught with inference-based questions that make children reason, not just recall.

  • Grade 1–2: Number sense, counting, addition & subtraction, shapes & patterns
  • Grade 3: Multiplication, division, measurement, introductory fractions
  • Grade 4: Multi-digit operations, equivalent fractions, area & perimeter
  • Grade 5: Decimals, ratios, data handling, geometry & problem-solving
  • Inference & adaptive reasoning questions woven into every session
50 min · twice weekly
📚
English
Grades 1–5 · Ages 6–11
Grades 1–5

Confident readers and expressive writers. We develop language skills from phonics and decoding in early grades through to comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, and structured writing — always with space for the child’s own voice.

  • Grade 1–2: Phonics, sight words, fluency reading, basic sentence construction
  • Grade 3: Reading comprehension, parts of speech, paragraph writing
  • Grade 4: Narrative & descriptive writing, vocabulary expansion, punctuation
  • Grade 5: Essay structure, persuasive writing, literary analysis
  • Monthly reading challenges with tutor-led group discussions
50 min · twice weekly
🌱
Telugu
Grades 1–5 · All levels
Kids & Adults

From first letters to fluent conversation. Our Telugu programme covers script, grammar, reading, and speaking — and connects children to their cultural identity through language. Adult classes focus on conversational fluency and heritage reconnection.

  • Beginners: Telugu script (Varnamala), basic vocabulary, simple sentences
  • Intermediate: Reading passages, grammar, spoken expression
  • Advanced: Composition, poetry, cultural context
  • Adults: Conversational Telugu, professional fluency, heritage reconnection
50 min · twice weekly
🆕
Hindi
Grades 1–5 · All levels
Kids & Adults

Devanagari script, spoken fluency, and cultural richness. Hindi is one of the world’s most widely spoken languages — and we make it accessible, engaging, and meaningful for every age group, from complete beginners to confident speakers.

  • Beginners: Devanagari script, basic vocabulary, simple conversations
  • Intermediate: Grammar, reading comprehension, written expression
  • Advanced: Essay writing, spoken fluency, Bollywood & cultural texts
  • Adults: Conversational Hindi, professional communication
50 min · twice weekly
💕
Sanskrit
Grades 1–5 · Adult learners
Kids & Adults

The mother of languages. Sanskrit builds a child’s vocabulary across multiple modern languages, sharpens analytical thinking, and connects them to a 5,000-year-old tradition. We start with the basics — script, pronunciation, simple shlokas — and build progressively.

  • Beginners: Devanagari script, basic Sanskrit alphabet, pronunciation
  • Grade 1–3: Simple vocabulary, numbers, body parts, nature words
  • Grade 4–5: Basic grammar (vibhakti), simple sentences, shlokas
  • Adults: Conversational Sanskrit, Vedic texts, grammar deepdive
50 min · twice weekly
🤖
AI for Kids
Grades 1–5 · Ages 6–11
✨ New

The patient teacher who is always there — answering every “but why?” at any hour, turning curiosity into something you can hold in your hands. Tutor-led, age-appropriate, and genuinely fun. Can be taken as a standalone course or as an add-on to any subject.

  • Ages 8–9: Pattern recognition, how Google knows what you want, AI in daily life

  • Hands-on making, curiosity-driven questions, real-world problems in every session

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Five students is the sweet spot between the social energy of a group and the personal attention of a 1-on-1 session. Every child speaks in every session, every child is heard, and the tutor can track what each student understands in real time. It is a deliberate, non-negotiable decision.

Group classes (max 5 students, $25/lesson) offer structured curriculum-based learning with the energy of peers. Private classes ($35–$40/lesson, 1-on-1) are fully personalised to your child — pace, schedule, and content are built around them specifically. Private classes are ideal for children on a Foundation Programme or who need intensive attention. The exact private class rate is confirmed at enrolment based on grade and subject.

After enrolment, your child completes a structured diagnostic assessment online — matched to their grade and subject. We test how they think, not just what they know. We look at reasoning patterns, where thinking breaks down, and what that tells us about how they learn. You receive a full written report and a personal call with your tutor to walk through the findings together.

If the assessment reveals knowledge gaps, we recommend a personalised Foundation Programme — 8 private 1-on-1 sessions focused specifically on fixing those gaps before joining a regular or advanced group batch. You can choose to take it up or decline — no pressure. We simply want every child to be ready and confident before they join a group.

All classes are held on Google Meet. Your child needs any device with a working camera and microphone — a laptop or tablet works best.
