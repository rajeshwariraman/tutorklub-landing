Privacy Policy

How we handle information about you and your child.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information TutorKlub collects when you use tutorklub.com, book a class or study with us, and how we use it. If you have any question, write to hello@tutorklub.com.

Who we are

TutorKlub is an online tutoring service run from Hyderabad, India. We teach children and adults through live classes on Google Meet.

What we collect

  • Booking details: the parent’s name, email address, phone number, and the child’s grade and subject, entered when you book a demo through Cal.com.
  • Messages: what you send us by WhatsApp or email.
  • Student records: a student login ID, assessment results, class progress and tutor notes, stored in our learning portal.
  • Class recordings: sessions are recorded so students can catch up on a missed class.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not see or store your full card details.
  • Website data: basic, anonymous visit statistics from our hosting provider.

Children’s information

Most of our students are children. We collect a child’s information only from, and with the agreement of, a parent or guardian. Students log in to the portal with a login ID, so parents’ phone numbers are not shown to other students or families. A parent can ask to see, correct or delete their child’s information at any time by emailing us.

How we use it

  • To arrange demos and classes, and to send class links and recordings.
  • To teach your child and track their progress.
  • To take payment and answer your questions.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

Services we use

We rely on trusted providers to run TutorKlub: Cal.com (booking), Google Meet (classes), Supabase (our learning portal), Razorpay (payments), WhatsApp and email (messages), Vercel (website hosting), and AI tools that help tutors prepare lessons and track progress. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep student records while your child studies with us. Ask us to delete them when you leave, and we will, except for records we must keep by law, such as payment records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information, or stop messages from us, by emailing hello@tutorklub.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
