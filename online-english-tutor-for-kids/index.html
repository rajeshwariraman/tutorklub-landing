English tutoring: common questions

Which grades do you teach?

Grades 1 to 5 (ages 6–11).

Does my child get help with writing?

Yes. Writing runs through every grade, from sentences in Grades 1–2 to essays and persuasive writing in Grade 5.

How many students are in a class?

A maximum of 5. Private one-to-one classes are also available.

What if my child misses a class?

Every session is recorded and the link is sent to you within 24 hours, so your child can catch up.

How much do the classes cost?

Fees depend on the plan and are shared on the free demo call. There is no contract; you pay per lesson.
