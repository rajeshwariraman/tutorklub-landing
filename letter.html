From Our Founder

A Letter to
Every Parent

Every child deserves to be understood before they are taught.

Dear Parent,

Thank you for visiting TutorKlub.

Before you decide whether we’re the right place for your child, I’d like to tell you why TutorKlub exists.

As a child, I believed I wasn’t good at studies.

Not because I didn’t want to learn — I just couldn’t understand the way I was being taught. Over time, I began to carry a quiet belief that learning came naturally to everyone else, but not to me.

That belief was wrong. But nobody told me that for a long time.

What changed wasn’t that I suddenly became smarter. Someone simply took the time to teach me differently. For the first time, things made sense. And as understanding came, something I hadn’t expected came with it — confidence.

Looking back, my life didn’t change because I became better at studies.

It changed because I stopped believing I wasn’t capable.

That shift opened doors I once thought were closed to me. Opportunities I had quietly ruled out became possible. A career I couldn’t have imagined began to take shape.

But the greatest lesson from that journey had nothing to do with grades or achievements.

It was this: every child can learn — when they’re taught in a way that works for them.

Over the years, I’ve met many children who are bright, curious and full of potential — yet have quietly convinced themselves they are “not good at Maths,” “not good at English,” or simply “not good at studies.”

Children who ask good questions in class but go quiet when they get something wrong. Children whose eyes say they understand more than their answers show.

I don’t think the problem is ability.

I think too many children are taught before they are understood.

That is why I started TutorKlub.

We don’t begin with a syllabus. We begin with the child. We take the time to understand how they think, where they’re getting stuck, and what they need to move forward — not just in their subject, but in how they feel about learning itself.

Our goal isn’t simply to improve marks. It’s to help children enjoy learning, think independently, and ask questions without fear.

Because when a child believes in themselves, better grades are often just the beginning.

Thank you for considering TutorKlub. If we can be the people who teach your child differently — the way someone once did for me — it would be a privilege.

With warm regards,

Rajeshwari R.
Founder, TutorKlub
