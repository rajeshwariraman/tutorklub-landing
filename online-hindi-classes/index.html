Why a small live class works better than an app

Children talk to each other in Hindi in class, which an app can’t offer.

With five students at most, the tutor can hear and correct every child, every session. Every session is recorded and the link is sent to you within 24 hours, so a missed class is never lost.

Hindi classes: common questions

My child has never learned Hindi. Can they join?

Yes. Beginners start with the Devanagari script, basic vocabulary and simple conversations.

Do you teach Hindi to adults?

Yes. Adult classes (ages 15+) are 60 minutes on a flexible schedule and focus on practical communication and confidence in speaking.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Every class is live with a tutor on Google Meet. Each session is also recorded and the link is sent to you within 24 hours.

How many students are in a class?

A maximum of 5. Private one-to-one classes are also available.

How much do the classes cost?

Fees depend on the plan and are shared on the free demo call. There is no contract; you pay per lesson.
